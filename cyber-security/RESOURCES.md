# Resources

Grouped by purpose. Doing beats reading in this field, so start with the hands-on platforms — the reading and video are for context and staying current. Everything here is free unless noted.

## Hands-on learning (start here)

- **PortSwigger Web Security Academy** — the best free web-security training anywhere, from the makers of Burp Suite. Pairs directly with the [Vulnerable Web App Pentest Lab](web-vuln-lab/) project.
- **OverTheWire — Bandit** — browser-based wargame that locks in Linux command-line and SSH fundamentals. The ideal first stop.
- **PicoCTF** — CTF platform built by Carnegie Mellon specifically for students; gentle difficulty curve, always-available practice gym.
- **TryHackMe** — guided beginner rooms and learning paths; issues completion certificates worth collecting.
- **Hack The Box** — Starting Point and free machines; public profile is linkable evidence.
- **VulnHub** — downloadable vulnerable VMs that drop straight into the lab (or onto the Pi).
- **Root Me** — large library of bite-sized challenges across categories.
- **OWASP Top 10** — the reference to map every web finding against in write-ups.

## Reading — staying current

- **The Hacker News** and **BleepingComputer** — daily security news.
- **Krebs on Security** — deep, readable investigative writing; great for seeing how real attacks unfold.
- **The Register** — tech and security news with a British slant.
- **SANS Internet Storm Center** and **SANS reading room** — free, more technical/academic.
- **2600: The Hacker Quarterly** — the classic print magazine; more about culture and ethos than tutorials. (Websites above stay more current.)

## Video

- **John Hammond** — approachable CTF walkthroughs and malware analysis.
- **NetworkChuck** — energetic, beginner-friendly networking and Linux.
- **LiveOverflow** — deeper and more technical; good once the basics are in place.

## UK student opportunities (high value on applications)

- **CyberFirst overview (NCSC)** — the UK's national programme, run by the NCSC (part of GCHQ).
- **CyberFirst free courses** — thousands of free places for 12–17 year-olds at universities/colleges across the UK. He's eligible now as a sixth former.
- Look up **CyberFirst Horizons** for his region — fully-funded summer work-experience programmes; deadlines fall in spring.
- **Note for later:** the university bursary (up to £24,000 over a degree, plus paid summer placements) is moving to the new **TechFirst / Tech Grad** programme under DSIT — check current NCSC/GCHQ pages when he reaches undergraduate applications.

## Programmes and deadlines (UK, ages ~16)

These recur every year on a predictable cycle. Most summer courses take applications in late winter to spring for that summer, so the move is to set calendar reminders now. Always confirm current dates on the official page — exact windows shift year to year, and delivery is moving from CyberFirst toward the new TechFirst programme under DSIT.

Legend: 🟢 do now / runs in the academic year · 🟡 apply ~Feb–Apr for the following summer · 💷 paid

**🟢 Right now — academic-year competitions (highest priority this term)**

- **CyberCenturion** — free team competition (ages 12–18), online rounds over autumn/winter, hardening Linux/Windows systems. Needs a team of 4 + a reserve and an adult team leader, so organise through school early in the term. Plays directly to Kali/Linux skills. Search "CyberCenturion" for the current cycle's registration.
- **WorldSkills UK — Cyber Security** — registration opens ~early March, online entry stage ~April, national finals later in the year. [worldskillsuk.org/competitions/cyber-security](https://worldskillsuk.org/competitions/cyber-security)

**🟡 Free national programme — CyberFirst (apply ~spring for summer)**

Free, delivered via regional partners (Smallpeice Trust / QA) with the NCSC. Best value and strong on applications.

- **CyberFirst Futures** — free 5-day course, ages 15–16.
- **CyberFirst Advanced** — free week-long course, ages 16–17 (Year 12); for those doing computing at AS/A-level or with a strong interest.
- **CyberFirst Advanced Wales** — free week-long summer course for 16–17 year-olds studying in Wales. [cyberfirstwales.co.uk](https://cyberfirstwales.co.uk)
- **CyberFirst Horizons (Newcastle)** — fully-funded 3-week summer programme for Year 12; small bursary + travel expenses; spring deadline. [cybernorth.biz/cyberfirst-horizons](https://cybernorth.biz/cyberfirst-horizons)
- Start here to find the right regional route: [ncsc.gov.uk/cyberfirst/overview](https://ncsc.gov.uk/cyberfirst/overview)

**Self-paced primer**

- **Introduction to Cybersecurity Essentials (Coursera)** — self-paced course covering core cyber security concepts; a good on-ramp before diving into the hands-on platforms above. [coursera.org/learn/introduction-to-cybersecurity-essentials](https://www.coursera.org/learn/introduction-to-cybersecurity-essentials?specialization=cybersecurity-fundamentals)

- **Local Hackspaces** and **DEF CON ("DC") groups** — supervised, real-world way to meet people in the field.
